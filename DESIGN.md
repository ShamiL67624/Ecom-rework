## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a luxury brutalist editorial philosophy tailored for high-end avant-garde streetwear. It merges raw architectural precision with high-fashion editorial restraint. The visual language conveys exclusivity, raw tactility, unapologetic edge, and understated luxury.

Key pillars of the aesthetic:
- **Severe Minimalism Meets Utilitarian Structure:** Surfaces rely on razor-sharp divisions, monospaced metadata cues, and sweeping negative space.
- **Deep Obsidian Immersion:** True dark mode architecture built from pure carbon, jet black, and charcoal gradations, avoiding neon distractions.
- **Warm Bronze / Cognac Tactility:** Metallic, patinated accents reminiscent of premium hardware, heavy zippers, and aged leather.
- **Strict Structural Discipline:** Zero border radii across interactive elements, strict hairline borders, and stark photographic framing.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around calibrated levels of dark value and tactile metallic accents:

- **Canvas & Backgrounds:**
  - `surface-canvas`: `#0c0c0c` (Absolute deep obsidian baseline)
  - `surface-panel`: `#141414` (Elevated container and sheet layer)
  - `surface-hover`: `#1a1a1a` (Hover states and tertiary structural fills)
- **Borders & Dividers:**
  - `border-subtle`: `#1f1f1d` (Internal cell dividers and muted structural grids)
  - `border-bold`: `#262624` (Prominent card containers, boundaries, inputs)
  - `border-accent`: `#c49a6c` (Focus rings and active selection borders)
- **Text & Foregrounds:**
  - `text-primary`: `#f5f5f3` (Crisp, warm chalk white for maximum clarity)
  - `text-secondary`: `#e5e5e0` (Body copy, high-readability muted white)
  - `text-tertiary`: `#9a9a94` (Muted steel metadata, timestamps, SKU markers)
  - `text-dim`: `#73736e` (Placeholders, inactive tabs, architectural tags)
- **Accents:**
  - `accent-primary`: `#c49a6c` (Warm bronze / cognac for primary calls-to-action, key badges)
  - `accent-muted`: `#a87e52` (Deep cognac for hover transitions, active badges)

## Typography

The type scale relies entirely on Plus Jakarta Sans, tuned to mimic editorial layout practices. 

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy:** Headlines lean into ultra-tight letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) with compressed line heights, producing dense, monument-like typographic headers.
- **Editorial Categorization:** All navigational tiers, SKUs, edition numbers, and meta attributes must render in uppercase through `label-caps` with wide letter-spacing (`0.14em`).
- **Body Balance:** Body text leverages comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) in natural case to preserve reading comfort against the dark, high-contrast palette.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes an editorial fluid grid model with high-tension framing inspired by broadsheet editorial publications:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column modular grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` outer margins. Content spreads often bleed entirely to edges via border containment.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` outer canvas padding.
- **Grid Treatment:** Structural borders directly trace grid lines. Columns and rows lock together seamlessly with visible, hairline divisions rather than floating island cards.

## Elevation & Depth

True to brutalist fundamentals, this design system rejects blurry drop shadows and faux realistic skeuomorphism:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Hard Planes:** Elevation is established entirely through subtle shift in surface tone (`#0c0c0c` to `#141414` to `#1a1a1a`) bounded by 1px hairlines (`#1f1f1d` and `#262624`).
- **No Diffusion Shadows:** `box-shadow` values are strictly `none`. 
- **Z-Index Layering:** Modals, quick-view drawers, and navigation overlays slide over bottom planes with a hard 1px outline on separating edges and a `#0c0c0c` solid backdrop at 90% opacity (no backdrop blur).
- **Active / Pressed Offset:** Interactive elements employ 0px transforms; active states rely purely on color inversion or sharp hairline illumination via `#c49a6c`.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

- Every corner across the system—buttons, inputs, modal drawers, badges, tooltips, and image frames—is cut at `0px` radius.
- Curves and pills are completely prohibited. Visual softness is strictly achieved through photography and warm cognac accent balance, never rounded corners.
- Hairline borders are uniformly 1px in stroke thickness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#c49a6c`, text `#0c0c0c`, 0px border radius, font `label-caps`, uppercase. Hover: background `#a87e52`. Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `transparent`, text `#f5f5f3`, 1px solid border `#262624`. Hover: background `#141414`, border color `#c49a6c`.
- **Ghost:** Background `transparent`, text `#9a9a94`, no border. Hover: text `#f5f5f3`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#141414`, text `#f5f5f3`, border 1px solid `#262624`.
- Padding: `0.75rem 1rem`. Typography: `body-md`.
- Placeholder color: `#73736e`.
- Focus state: border-color `#c49a6c`, outline `none`.
- Error state: border-color `#b93838`, with accompanying micro-label in `label-sm`.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- Framed by 1px `#1f1f1d` borders. Background `#141414`.
- Image containers feature full bleed edge-to-edge alignment with a zero-radius bounding box.
- Metadata bars run beneath imagery with visible top-border separation (`1px solid #1f1f1d`), positioning size runs, inventory indicators, and edition numbers using `label-caps` in `#9a9a94`.

### Chips & Status Tags
- Zero border radius. Background `#0c0c0c` with 1px solid `#262624`.
- Typography: `label-caps` in `#f5f5f3`.
- Selected/Active state: background `#c49a6c`, text `#0c0c0c`, border 1px solid `#c49a6c`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom 16px square boxes with 0px corner radius.
- Unchecked: 1px border `#262624`, background `#0c0c0c`.
- Checked: 1px border `#c49a6c`, background `#c49a6c` with inner solid black square mark.

### Lists & Tables
- Border-collapse grid styling. Rows separated by 1px solid `#1f1f1d`.
- Headers set in `label-caps` with text `#73736e`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Hover row state shifts to `#141414`.

### Lookbook / Editorial Carousel Banners
- Architectural grid layouts grouping imagery and technical specifications side by side.
- Index indicators rendered in monospace-aligned numeric forms (`01 / 08`) using `label-caps` in `#c49a6c`.
